# Rost-West Mapgen

## Mapping of real-world

### Daytime

- day in game: 20 minutes
- day in reality: 1440 minutes
- scale factor: 72 game days per real day

### Walking speed

- Walking speed in game: 240 nodes/min = 4 nodes/s = 14400 nodes/h
- Waking speed reality: 5 km/h = 1.4 m/s
- factor: game is 2.9 × as fast as in reality

### Walking distance per day

- walking distance in game per game day: 4800 nodes / day
- walking distance in reality per real day: 120 km / 24 h = 120000 m/day
- ratio: 25

### Elevation

- Highest mountain in game: 256 nodes
- Highest mountain in reality: 8848 m
- ratio: 35 m / node

### Walk across the world

- 330 real days to cross the world
- 14 game days to cross the world
- ratio: 24

### Cloud levels

- mid level in real world is around 5000 m (2000 m - 7000 m depending on biome)
- cloud level in game: 64
